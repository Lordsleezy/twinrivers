What we install

Linear-screw and articulated-arm operators for swing gates. Chain or rack drive for slide gates. Keypads, vehicle remotes, intercom dry contacts, photo eyes, and edge sensors. Solar with battery backup on long ranch driveways where trenching power is not worth it.

What we repair

A motor will not save a sagging gate. We rehang the leaf, reset the hinge post, then service the opener—gears, boards, limit switches, photo eyes, and solar panels that stopped charging after a winter of foothill weather.

Where we do this work

Motorized gate installation and repair in Sacramento, Elk Grove, Folsom, Roseville, Rocklin, Granite Bay, Auburn, Grass Valley, and nearby Northern California communities. Same licensed crew that builds the fence line.

Frequently asked questions

Do you install motorized gates?

Yes. We install swing and slide operators, keypads, remotes, and safety sensors, and we repair existing motorized gates.

Can you add an opener to a gate you did not install?

Usually. We check hinge condition, post strength, and balance first. A sagging gate has to be rebuilt before a motor goes on.

Do you repair automatic gate openers?

Yes. We service motors, boards, limit switches, photo eyes, and solar kits on swing and slide gates.

Solar or hardwired?

Both. Long rural driveways often use solar with battery backup. Homes with nearby power usually go AC.
