## Import from Google Business Profile

Bing Places can import a verified Google Business Profile. That is the safest way to avoid a second, conflicting listing.

Do this after approval:

1. Sign in at https://www.bing.com/forbusiness with the Microsoft account that should own the listing.
2. Choose **Add a business** / **Import from Google**.
3. Select the official **Twin Rivers Fence** GBP (the one with website twinriversfence.com and phone (916) 906-2254).
4. If Bing already shows the Volcanoville panel, **claim that listing** instead of creating a new one.
5. Correct the city to Grass Valley / service-area. Do not add 21030 Home Camp Rd unless you want a public storefront.
6. Match hours to the official schedule you confirm in the GBP report (8–5 vs 9–5).
7. Upload the same authentic photos used on GBP.
8. Set website to https://twinriversfence.com/ only.
9. Do not create six city listings.
